Who is Eligible for Gratuity in India?
You are eligible for gratuity if you meet these below conditions:
- You have completed at least 5 years of continuous service with the employer
  (for resignation, retirement, or superannuation).
- The 5-year rule is waived in case of death or disablement due to accident or
  disease.
- New Rule (Labour Code 2025): Fixed-term employees are eligible for pro-rata
  gratuity after just 1 year of continuous service.
- The organization has 10 or more employees.
- Gratuity is payable on retirement, resignation, death, disablement, or
  termination (except for misconduct in some cases).
